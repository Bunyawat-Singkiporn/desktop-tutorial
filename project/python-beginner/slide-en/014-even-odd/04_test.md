## Problem

|condition| Output |
|----------|--------|
|Numbers **Even** and **> 20**| `Special` |
|other| `Normal` |

---

## example

**Input:**
```

```

**Output:**
```
Special
```

> 24 is an even number and 24 > 20 → Special
