**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy

---

## Task

Game score starts at 10 — update it to 25, then show before/after.

```python
player = {"name": "Hero", "score": 10}
```

**Output:**
```
Before: 10
After: 25
```

---

## 💡 Hint
